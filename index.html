About

Singapore

I work at the intersection of operations research, revenue management, and online algorithms, designing real-time decision rules with provable guarantees against unpredictable demand.

My research studies robust real-time decision-making under uncertain demand, spanning resource allocation, order scheduling, and dynamic pricing. The focus is on algorithms whose performance is guaranteed even when arrivals are adversarial or hard to forecast.

A second strand of my work combines real-time decisions with machine-learned predictions. The resulting algorithms exploit accurate forecasts when they are available and remain resilient when they are not.
